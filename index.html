Dead Ringer

Mystery Academy

• Chi4Vodka Distillery •

Welcome to Campanile Academy! You're going to be staying here for a long while, as there's nothing beyond the premises. Survive by fulfilling your needs, making connections, and keeping your door locked at night. When one of your classmates breaks under the pressure, it's up to you to solve the case.

  • A unique academy experience: every run features a procedurally-generated map and NPCs, with handcrafted rooms.
  • More than plot devices: Your classmates have their own personalities, hobbies, and drives. Their relationships and life continue without your involvement. Except when they're dead, of course.
  • Challenge your logic and attention skills: The longer you last, the trickier the mysteries will be. You can have your very own locked-room mystery to solve, no magic to be found here.
